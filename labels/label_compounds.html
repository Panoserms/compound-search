Compound sheet

All words are labelled. Click Download CSV and save the file as labels/compounds_labels.csv in the project folder.
How to label (about 20 seconds per word)
  1. Cut the word. Click between two letters where two parts meet. A linking element stays with the left part: Bundes|gericht, Sonnen|schein. Click again to remove a cut.
  2. Pick the type. The page then moves to the next word.
  3. Your work is saved in this browser as you go. You can close the page and come back.
